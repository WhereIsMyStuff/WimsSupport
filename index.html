Where Is My Stuff

Privacy Policy & Support · Effective November 7, 2026

The short version

Where Is My Stuff keeps everything on your iPhone. We don't collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no third-party tracking.

What the app stores

The app stores the following only on your device: the items you add (names, categories, locations, notes and photos), and the loans you record (who has an item, the dates, and any notes or condition photos). When you choose a person from your contacts, the app keeps a copy of that person's name, phone number and email so it can show them and pre-fill reminders. That copy is stored only in the app on your iPhone.

Permissions

  • Camera – used only when you tap “Take photo”. Photos are saved inside the app on your device.
  • Photo library – you choose individual photos using Apple's photo picker. The app only receives the photos you select.
  • Contacts – you choose people using Apple's contact picker. The app only receives the contacts you select. If you tap “Save to Contacts”, Apple's new-contact screen opens and you decide whether to save.
  • Notifications – used to remind you, on your device, when a loaned item is due. These are scheduled locally and are not sent through any server.

Messages and email

When you choose to remind a borrower, the app opens Apple's Messages or Mail with a pre-filled message. You review it and tap Send yourself. The message is sent by Apple's apps, not by us, and we never see it.

Sharing and tracking

We do not share your data with anyone, we do not track you across apps or websites, and the app makes no network connections of its own.

Deleting your data

You can delete any item, loan or person inside the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its data. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing stored on our side to delete.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Support
